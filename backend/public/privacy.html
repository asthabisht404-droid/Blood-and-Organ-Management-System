Privacy Policy

Your trust is paramount. This policy details how we handle and protect your critical personal data.

Preamble

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information with the highest standards of security and confidentiality. This policy ensures transparency regarding the collection, use, and protection of data within the Emergency Blood & Organ System.

1. Information We Collect

We strictly limit data collection to necessary fields, including your full name, contact details (phone/email), blood/organ type, and location. This data is collected solely to facilitate safe and timely medical connections between donors and recipients.

2. Data Utilization

Your data is used exclusively to **match donors and patients accurately**, maintain compliant medical records, and continuously improve system efficiency and response times. We guarantee that we will **never share your data publicly** or with third-party marketers without your explicit, informed consent.

3. Data Security and Protection

All user data is **securely encrypted** both in transit and at rest. We employ modern, industry-standard protection measures and regularly audit our security protocols to safeguard against unauthorized access or data breaches.

4. Your Data Rights

You maintain full control over your personal data. You have the right to **access, correct, or request the deletion** of your personal data at any time by formally contacting our dedicated administrative team via the designated channels.
