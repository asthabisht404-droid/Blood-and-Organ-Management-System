Official Donor Policy and Commitment

This document outlines the mandatory ethical, medical, and procedural standards required for participation in the Emergency Blood & Organ Donation System.

1. Donor Eligibility and Qualification

  • Age Requirement: Donors must be aged between 18 and 60 years at the point of registration or donation.
  • Medical Standard: Compliance with rigorous medical fitness and health standards verified by authorized personnel is mandatory.
  • Platform Integrity: The donor profile must be active, complete, and verified through the system's authentication process.

2. Donor Obligations and Data Accuracy

  • Data Integrity: Donors are required to ensure all health data, medical history, and contact information remains accurate and current on the system.
  • Response Mandate: Donors must commit to providing timely responses to official donation requests to ensure optimal patient outcomes.
  • Transparency: Full honesty and ethical disclosure are required in all platform interactions and record submissions.

3. Platform Security and Ethical Commitments

The organization guarantees the highest level of data security and confidentiality for all submitted personal and medical information. All donation procedures are managed under strict medical supervision and adhere to governing national and international ethical standards. Our commitment is to protect the welfare of the donor while optimizing the speed and efficacy of the lifesaving match process.
